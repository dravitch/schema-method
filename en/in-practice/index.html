Collective memory

Leave a trail the team can resume.

After a meeting, a SCHEMA brief separates what is acquired, what remains unknown and the next evidence needed. Team members do not have to repeat the work of consolidation and interpretation.

The marker after each meeting

✓

Acquired

Observed facts, available assets and decisions actually made.

???

Unknowns

Open questions, dependencies, divergences and scope limits.

→

Next evidence

The bounded verification most capable of changing the next decision.

◇

Decision sought

Proceed, test, bound, pivot, suspend or stop a trajectory.

From one milestone to the next

01

Initial promise

What the project claims it can make possible.

02

Current qualification

What is demonstrated now and where the path breaks.

03

Evidence loops

One to three preregistered checks with decision criteria.

04

Next milestone

An honest presentation of demonstrated value, limitations and the investment requested.

The pitch is not the final proof. It is a decision point that should show what exists, what remains to be verified and exactly what the next investment would fund.

A common language, not a second project-management layer

Technical profiles can specify properties and tests. Product, analysis, learning and management profiles can specify use, authority, action and value. The same path preserves the interfaces between those contributions.

A methodological breadcrumb trail. Each trace lets absent team members resume the reasoning without reconstructing the whole meeting and gradually feeds the decision record.
