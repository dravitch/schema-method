Origin, without attribution

The prototype was only one link.

The retrospective analysis of a technology project was meant to qualify a performance claim. It revealed a deeper question: how does that capability become a decision, an action and value?

The technical indicator was not enough to demonstrate the project's complete trajectory.

Scope

An aggregate result did not necessarily demonstrate the useful property under the decisive conditions.

Authority

Producing a signal, making a decision and taking action belonged to distinct responsibilities.

Value

The final outcome was not yet linked to an observable action and a shared indicator.

This case generated the SCHEMA hypothesis. It does not validate it. The method will be tested on contrasting projects under a protocol fixed before their results.

Strong hypothesis under test

No overall score, no automated decision.
