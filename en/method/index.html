See before scoring.

SCHEMA starts by making the initial state visible without its own vocabulary. The framework must not select the facts that will confirm it.

00

Discover

Make facts, actors, dependencies and unknowns visible without applying SCHEMA.

01

Map

Connect each link and each interface of the problem up to the value.

02

Qualify

Distinguish established, supported, indeterminate, contradictory and not testable.

03

Test

Pre-register at most three checks capable of changing the decision.

Separate decisions

The claim, the asset and the operational trajectory each receive their own decision.

Continue

The path required for the next step is supported.

Test

A decisive ignorance can be lifted by a bounded verification.

Bound

The claim must remain within its demonstrated domain.

Pivot

The asset is useful, but not in the announced trajectory.

Pause

A dependency or external decision is missing.

Halt

An established contradiction invalidates the considered trajectory.

Strong hypothesis under test

No overall score, no automated decision.
