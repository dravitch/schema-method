A decision discipline

See what is known. Organize what is missing.

SCHEMA does not force a binary answer. A declared unknown becomes an evidence program tied to a decision.

Discover

Collect facts, actors, assets, dependencies and unknowns in the project's own language.

Map

Connect every link from the problem to value and expose the breaks.

Qualify

Distinguish what is established, supported, indeterminate, contradictory or not testable.

Test

Select no more than three checks capable of changing the next decision.

Evidence only has meaning within its scope

StateWhat it supports
EstablishedPrimary evidence supports the property within the stated scope.
SupportedEvidence converges, but a verification or boundary remains open.
IndeterminateEvidence is missing in both directions; the next observation must be selected.
ContradictoryAdmissible sources conflict; the divergence must remain visible.
Not testableNo realistic observation can settle the claim as stated.

Decide without reducing the project to a score

Continue

The path required for the next step is supported.

Test

A decisive ignorance can be lifted by a bounded verification.

Bound

The claim must remain within its demonstrated domain.

Pivot

The asset is useful, but not in the announced trajectory.

Pause

A dependency or external decision is missing.

Halt

An established contradiction invalidates the considered trajectory.

No magic average. A strong technical metric cannot compensate for a missing user, authority, procedure or useful action.
