Pre-investment qualification

Before moving forward, what do we know?

SCHEMA checks whether the available evidence justifies the next investment. It replaces neither technical expertise nor project management.

A promise becomes qualifiable when the problem, evidence and decision can be connected to an observable action, outcome and value.

Two different questions

MANAGEMENT

Is the project progressing properly?

Plan, coordinate, deliver and monitor the work already decided.

SCHEMA

Does the evidence justify the next step?

Qualify what is demonstrated, what is missing and which test can change the decision.

A technical success is not yet an investment case

A prototype can perform well in an experiment without demonstrating that its output reaches the right person, supports a decision in time or produces an observable result.

Useful property

What capability has actually been demonstrated, and within which conditions?

Operational meaning

Who receives the result, what decision follows and what action becomes possible?

Retained value

What data, code, knowledge or use remains valuable if the initial application changes?

The project is not its first solution. A claim can be bounded, tested or abandoned without erasing the assets and uses that retain value.

From a promising result to a justified investment

SCHEMA structures the review by connecting a project's technical results with its intended operational use and measurable value. Strong technical performance matters, but the next investment decision also depends on how the output will be used, by whom and for what decision.

Importantly, SCHEMA separates the intrinsic value of a technical asset, such as a chatbot or an agentic orchestration, from the value of the particular application built around it. If evidence for the initial use case is insufficient because of data limitations, operational conditions or overlap with existing solutions, this does not necessarily reduce the value of the underlying work. It may point toward a more relevant application.

The intent is not to apply a pass/fail assessment. It is to make constructive feedback precise: what has been demonstrated, what remains uncertain and what evidence would best support the project's next investment decision.

The focus stays on the fundamentals: what the asset can inherently do, what remains invariant and what retains value when the surrounding use case changes.
