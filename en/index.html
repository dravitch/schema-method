Pre-investment qualification

From problem to value.

SCHEMA turns a promise into a verifiable path before resources are committed.

At every maturity level, an allocation must rest on a justifiable path or explicitly fund the experiment that can verify it.

The path

  1. 01
    Problem
    Does it exist independently of the solution?
  2. 02
    Scope
    What exactly is promised, and within what limits?
  3. 03
    Evidence
    Do the observations support the useful property?
  4. 04
    Procedure
    Is the result reproducible and interpretable?
  5. 05
    Decision
    Which mechanism synchronizes mandate, competence and availability in the useful window?
  6. 06
    Action
    What actually becomes possible to do?
  7. 07
    Outcome
    What observable change must follow?
  8. 08
    Value
    Why does this change justify the allocation?

What SCHEMA protects

Declared ignorance produces a program of evidence, never an automatic stop.

A technical metric does not compensate for a break in authority.

A claim can die without the asset dying.

The next piece of evidence is chosen for its ability to change the decision.

Corrections remain visible in the history of knowledge.
